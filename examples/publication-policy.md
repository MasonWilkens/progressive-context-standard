# Publication policy

**Purpose:** Define approval conditions for the imaginary report workflow.  
**Read when:** Determining whether a report may be released publicly.  
**Scope:** Public reports in this example only.  
**Role:** Illustrative policy, not an observation or real authorization.

A designated approver must approve the exact report version before publication. Approval of an earlier draft does not approve later changes.

The publisher records the approved version and release location. The layout reference does not grant approval.

If approval is absent or ambiguous, stop publication and contact the designated publication owner. No real owner is identified by this example.

For the workflow, read [Publish a report](publish-report.md) when preparing a release. This backlink is navigation, not a requirement to reopen the procedure recursively.
